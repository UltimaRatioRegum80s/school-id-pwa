Here's my read on what's making it feel flat, and how to fix it.
🔴 The core problem
Your layout is mobile-first stretched onto desktop. Single narrow column of content + huge empty right side = wasted real estate. Schools view this on laptops/projectors, so it needs to fill the width with purpose.
🟠 Biggest offenders (by screen)
Dashboard (Image 1)

KPI cards are enormous for a single number — lots of dead padding
Status Breakdown bars are mostly empty grey tracks (boring when 90% of data is in one row)
Recent Activity is a thin list floating in white space

Activities empty state (Image 2)

An entire blank screen for "no activities" is a missed chance — biggest offender for empty real estate

Students (Image 3)

Left list + giant empty right panel that does nothing until you click

Admin (Images 5/6)

Cards centred with massive margins left and right

🟢 High-impact fixes
1. Fill the canvas with a real grid

Dashboard → 2-column layout: KPIs + status on left, live activity feed + a chart on right
Students → clicking a name opens their profile in the right panel (timeline, behaviour, photo) instead of leaving it empty

2. Make data visual, not numeric

Replace the flat status bars with a donut/ring chart for attendance breakdown (Present / Late / Not Arrived) — instantly scannable
Add a small trend sparkline to each KPI ("vs yesterday")
Colour-code statuses consistently everywhere: 🟢 On Campus · 🟡 Late · 🔴 Not Arrived · ⚪ Checked Out

3. Turn empty states into value

"No activities" screen → show suggested templates ("Create Morning Assembly", "Add Detention") as clickable cards
Add a "Today at a glance" timeline on Activities even when empty

4. Density + hierarchy

Shrink KPI card padding ~40%, fit them in one tight row with icons
Add a subtle coloured left-border or icon tint per card so they're not all white
Use card shadows/section dividers so content doesn't float

5. The "must-have" hooks for schools

Live attendance % with a visual gauge (heads of year love this)
Quick-action bar (Scan / Mark Absent / Print Cards) always visible
Per-grade heatmap so staff see problem groups at a glance
